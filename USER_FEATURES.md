# VARC Trainer — What the User Gets

The full set of features a CAT aspirant will experience once all phases are done.
(Admin tools and behind-the-scenes infrastructure are not listed here — this is user-facing only.)

---

## Practice the core skill
- Short-paragraph RC questions designed around trap options
- 5 question types — inference, tone, title, detail, application — across 5 topics
- **Analysis mode** — pick an answer, explain your reasoning, get AI feedback on *how you thought*
- **Intuition mode** — fast, timed picking with option-elimination and a points system

## AI that coaches your thinking
- Reasoning scored 1–5 (not just right/wrong)
- Every answer explained: why the correct option is right, why the trap was tempting, and a key takeaway
- **AI Reading Coach** — paste any article, get AI-generated questions, then a back-and-forth Socratic conversation where the AI challenges your reasoning and guides you to the answer without giving it away

## Flexible ways to answer
- Type your reasoning
- **Speak it** — voice-to-text with live transcription you can edit
- **Quote the passage** — highlight a line and drop it into your reasoning as a citation

## Sessions your way
- Choose question count, timer mode (untimed / count-up / countdown), and scope
- Skip questions; sessions survive a refresh
- In timed sessions, get all your AI feedback in a clean review at the end (uninterrupted flow)

## Bring your own content
- Add your own CAT passages + questions (write them manually, or have AI draft them from a passage you paste)
- See, edit, and delete your questions; practice them in your sessions

## Track and improve
- Dashboard: accuracy, trap-pick rate, reasoning score, accuracy-by-type chart
- Your biggest weakness identified ("most dangerous trap", "weakest type")
- Expandable history of past attempts with full feedback
- Coach stats: accuracy + how many exchanges you needed (lower = sharper reasoning)

## Stay consistent
- **Spaced repetition** — automatically re-serves the trap types you keep failing
- **Streaks & daily goals** — daily streak, goal progress, gentle nudges

## Account & experience
- Sign up / log in, change password, reset your data
- Clean redesigned UI with dark mode
- Works on mobile

## Plans
- Free tier to start; paid tier unlocks unlimited practice, the Coach, AI question generation, and voice

---

*In one sentence:* a CAT VARC platform with two trainers (short-paragraph drilling + full-article Socratic coaching), three input methods (click, voice, quote-cited reasoning), AI feedback that grades thinking not just answers, your own content, and spaced-repetition + streaks to keep you coming back.
